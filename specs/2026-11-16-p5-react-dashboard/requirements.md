# Phase 5 Requirements - React dashboard

## Goal
A trading dashboard a broker's staff would recognise: price board, depth, ticket, margin health, audit.

## Scope
- Login with role-aware navigation (Prices, Trading, Portfolio, Margin, Statement, Liquidity, Risk, Audit)
- Price board with sortable columns, offer and bid shading, change heat-map, trend sparklines and price flash
- Trading: symbol list, instrument header, order ticket with live pre-trade hints, orders with cancel, trades tape, depth with volume bars
- Margin: gauge against the maintenance line, margin call banner; risk officer sees the margin-call list; admin sees the audit trail with filter
- Toasts for outcomes; dark mode follows the system; responsive below 1100px
